Employee Credit and Debit Points

Ensure secure and efficient tracking of employee performance using the guidelines for credit and debit points. These rules help in maintaining a productive and disciplined work environment.

Credit Points

  • Employees are awarded credit points for adhering to the following practices:
  • 1. Punctuality: Arriving at work on time consistently.
  • 2. Work Reporting: Regularly reporting daily work progress.
  • 3. Timely Updates: Updating the coordinator’s report as scheduled.
  • 4. Task Completion: Completing assigned tasks by the due date.
  • 5. Revenue-Generating Initiatives: Successfully completing any revenue-generating plans will earn the employee an additional credit point.
  • 6. Attendance: Avoiding uninformed absences.
  • 7. Asset Care: Proper maintenance of office assets and resources.
  • 8. Professional Communication: Answering official calls courteously and professionally.
  • 9. Discipline: Maintaining discipline within office and garden areas.
  • 10. Dress Code Compliance: Wearing proper formal attire during weekdays.

Debit Points

  • Debit points are issued for any non-compliance with the above standards:
  • 1. Late Arrival or habitual tardiness.
  • 2. Lack of Work Reporting or irregular reporting.
  • 3. Delayed Updates: Failing to update the coordinator’s report on time.
  • 4. Incomplete Tasks: Not completing assigned tasks by the due date.
  • 5. Uninformed Absences.
  • 6. Negligence with Office Assets: Failing to maintain or damaging office property.
  • 7. Improper Communication: Unprofessional handling of official calls.
  • 8. Disciplinary Issues: Any form of misconduct or breach of decorum within office premises.
  • 9. Dress Code Violations: Not adhering to formal dress code requirements.
